## What to Check Before You Sign Up

Free plans look similar on a features page and very different once your leads are inside. Before you pick a free CRM for small business use, check these five limits against how your team works today:

- **Users.** Count everyone who will touch a lead, including the owner who checks the pipeline once a week. A 1-user plan means one login, not one active seller.
- **Records.** Some plans count contacts only, others count every deal, note and task. Bigin's 500 records cover pipelines, contacts, activities, notes, companies and products together, so they fill up faster than they sound.
- **WhatsApp.** If customers reply on WhatsApp, a CRM without it leaves the most important conversations on someone's phone.
- **Automations.** Even 3 automations can send a reminder when a lead goes quiet or move a deal when a stage changes.
- **Exports.** Make sure you can take your data out. Zoho CRM's free edition allows export up to 10 times, and Privyr's free plan limits CSV exports to 100 rows.

Write your answers down, then compare them with the table above. The best free plan is the one whose limits you will not hit in the next six months.

## 2. Zoho CRM Free Edition

Zoho calls this edition "free forever, for 3 users", with no trial period and no forced upgrade. It is the most generous free plan here for a small team that needs proper CRM records.

**Free plan limits:** 3 users, 5,000 records, 1 GB of file storage, workflow automation, up to 10 email templates, tasks, meetings and calls, standard reports, 5,000 API calls a day and mobile apps. Data import is free, and export is allowed up to 10 times.

**Not included:** multiple pipelines, custom fields, email integration, WhatsApp, built-in calling, dashboards and forecasting.

**When you upgrade:** Standard is ₹800 per user per month billed yearly (₹1,300 monthly). See our [Zoho CRM alternative](/zoho-crm-alternative) page for a side-by-side with TracktCRM.

## 3. Bigin Free

Bigin is Zoho's pipeline-first CRM for small businesses, and its free plan is a clean starting point for one person. After Bigin's 15-day trial, accounts move to the Free plan rather than being charged.

**Free plan limits:** 1 user, 500 records (counted across pipelines, contacts, activities, notes, companies and products), a single pipeline, 3 automations, a standard dashboard and apps for iOS, iPadOS, Android and macOS.

**Not included:** email and WhatsApp integration (both from Express), custom fields, mass email, social lead ads and AI.

**When you upgrade:** Express is ₹400 per user per month billed yearly (₹550 monthly), with 50,000 records, 3 pipelines and 30 automations. ## 4. HubSpot Free CRM

HubSpot's free tools are "free for up to 2 users" with no expiry date. They are strongest for teams that live in Gmail or Outlook.

**Free plan limits:** 2 users, 1,000 contacts (plus up to 1 million other records), the default deal pipeline, Gmail and Outlook connection, 3 email templates, 10 reporting dashboards and the mobile app.

**Not included:** custom pipelines, sales workflows, calling minutes and WhatsApp, which needs Marketing Hub or Service Hub Professional or above. Emails sent from the free tools carry HubSpot branding.

**When you upgrade:** Starter showed ₹585 per seat per month billed yearly as a new-customer offer, against a list price of ₹1,670. HubSpot says the offer can end at any time. Compare it on our [HubSpot alternative](/hubspot-alternative) page.

## 5. Kylas Embark

Kylas is an Indian sales CRM, and its Embark plan is labelled "Lifetime Free" with unlimited users.

**Free plan limits:** unlimited users, 5 GB of storage, lead and deal pipelines, reports and a sales dashboard, mobile apps, quotations and lead capture from portals such as IndiaMART and Justdial. The record limit is capped, but the pricing page shows both 10,000 and 1,000 records in different places.

**Not included:** WhatsApp, workflow automation, Gmail, Outlook or SMTP email, telephony and custom fields.

**When you upgrade:** Elevate is a flat ₹12,999 a month for unlimited users. That is good value for 15 or more people and expensive for 2.

## 6. Privyr Free Forever

Privyr is a mobile-first lead app for solo sellers and small teams who get leads from ads and portals.

**Free plan limits:** up to 3 team members, unlimited lead source integrations, instant lead alerts in the app, up to 10 client groups and 10 templates. You can call, text, email or WhatsApp a lead in a few taps.

**The catch:** the free plan only lets you engage and track your most recent lead. Follow-up sequences, the timeline and file tracking work for that one lead, and WhatsApp automation add-ons are paid.

**When you upgrade:** Pro is US$10 per user per month billed yearly, or US$22 monthly.

## 7. Bitrix24 Free

Bitrix24 bundles CRM with tasks, chat and other workplace tools, and its free plan has no end date.

**Free plan limits:** 1,000 deals, 1,000 contacts and 1,000 companies, 1 pipeline, 5 automation rules and 5 GB of storage. User limits conflict on Bitrix24's own pages: the comparison table and FAQ say unlimited users, while the plan card says "100% free for 1–2 users".

**Not included:** WhatsApp, email integration, telephony, CRM forms and the leads module.

**When you upgrade:** Basic is Rs 1,910 per organisation per month billed yearly, for 5 users.

## 8. Vtiger One Pilot

Vtiger's One Pilot is a "forever free" plan with more seats than most.

**Free plan limits:** up to 10 users, 3,000 records, 3 GB of storage, a sales pipeline with Kanban view, workflows, email and phone integration, Google and Office 365 add-ins and a mobile app.

**Not included:** multiple pipelines, lead routing, customisable dashboards and AI. WhatsApp is not listed on the pricing page.

**When you upgrade:** One Growth is ₹840 per user per month billed yearly (₹1,050 monthly).

## When Free Stops Being Enough

A free CRM for small business is a good way to build the habit of logging every lead. These are the signs it is time to pay:

1. **A second person starts selling.** TracktCRM and Bigin are free for 1 user. If two people share one login, you lose track of who promised what.
2. **You hit the record cap.** 500 records on Bigin or 1,000 contacts on HubSpot can fill up in a few months of steady enquiries.
3. **Customers reply on WhatsApp.** If chats live on one phone, deals get lost when that person is on leave. Most free plans exclude WhatsApp.
4. **Follow-ups slip.** If you are copying the same reminder every day, an automation should send it.
5. **You need reports for decisions.** Free dashboards show counts. Paid plans show which source brings won deals.
