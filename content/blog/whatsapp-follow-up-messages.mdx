A WhatsApp follow-up message is a short note you send to a lead or customer to move a conversation forward: after an enquiry, a missed call, a quote, a site visit or a payment due date. In India it is often the fastest way to get a reply, because buyers check WhatsApp long before they open email.

Below are 25 follow-up messages grouped by situation, with template-ready versions for when the 24-hour window has closed. First, though, a problem that quietly costs many sales teams more deals than any badly written message: running customer chats on salespeople's personal phones.

<KeyTakeaway title="Five rules for WhatsApp follow-ups that get replies">

- **One message, one question.** Short messages get answered, long ones get "seen".
- **Name the thing.** Mention the quote, product, visit date or invoice, not just "following up".
- **Reply in minutes, follow up in days.** Fast first replies, then patient follow-ups.
- **Use a business number, not a personal phone,** so chats stay with the company and the lead.
- **Respect the rules.** Get permission, stop when asked, and use approved templates after 24 hours on the Business Platform.

</KeyTakeaway>

## Why WhatsApp Follow-Ups on Personal Phones Cost You Sales

Most small sales teams start the same way: each rep uses their own WhatsApp to chat with leads. It feels fast and free. The costs only show up later.

- **Chats leave when people leave.** When a rep resigns, months of customer conversations, prices quoted and promises made go with their phone.
- **Managers cannot see what is happening.** There is no way to check whether a hot lead got a reply, what price was offered or why a deal went quiet.
- **Follow-ups depend on memory.** A chat that scrolls below fifty others is a follow-up that never happens.
- **Replies stop when the rep is busy.** On leave, in a meeting or off duty, nobody else can answer the customer.
- **Customer data sits on personal devices.** Phone numbers, documents and payment details mix with family chats, outside any company control.
- **Duplicate and conflicting replies.** Two reps message the same lead with different prices, which looks unprofessional and invites bargaining.

None of this shows up in a report, which is why it is easy to ignore. It shows up as leads that "went cold" and customers who say "I never heard back".

### A 5-question audit for your team

Answer these honestly. If you say "no" to two or more, WhatsApp is leaking sales.

1. If your best salesperson left tomorrow, would you still have every customer chat?
2. Can you see, right now, which WhatsApp leads have not had a reply today?
3. Does someone get reminded when a lead goes quiet for three days?
4. When a rep is on leave, can a colleague pick up their chats with full context?
5. Is every WhatsApp lead saved in one place with its source, owner and stage?

## WhatsApp Rules Every Sales Team Should Know

These rules come from Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy and developer documentation. Breaking them can get your number restricted.

- **Get permission first.** You may only message people who gave you their number and agreed to hear from your business. A tick box on your enquiry form that names your company and says you will contact them on WhatsApp is a simple way to do it.
- **Honour opt-outs.** If someone says stop, stop, on WhatsApp or anywhere else.
- **The 24-hour customer service window.** On the Business Platform, when a customer messages or calls you, a 24-hour window opens. Each new message from them resets it. Inside it you can send normal messages. Once it closes, you can only send pre-approved template messages.
- **Templates are reviewed by Meta.** Each template is approved before you can use it and given a category: marketing, utility or authentication. Utility templates relate to something the customer asked for or bought, with no promotional content. Most sales nudges count as marketing.
- **Template variables.** Templates use placeholders such as `{{1}}` for the customer's name or the quote number. Do not start or end a template with a variable, and keep enough fixed text around each one for the reviewer to understand the message.
- **Automation needs a human path.** If a bot replies, customers must be able to reach a person, by chat, phone or email.

## How to Move Sales Follow-Ups Off Personal Phones

1. **Get one business number for sales.** Use the WhatsApp Business App to start, and move to the Business Platform when more than a few people need access or you need templates and automation.
2. **Connect it to your CRM.** Every chat should save on the lead, next to calls, emails and notes.
3. **Give every lead an owner.** Assign new WhatsApp leads automatically so nobody waits while the team decides who replies.
4. **Write your templates once.** Use the 25 below as a starting point, edit them to sound like your business and submit the follow-up ones for approval.
5. **Set reminders on WhatsApp activity.** If a lead has not replied in three days, the owner should get a follow-up task automatically.
6. **Review weekly.** Look at unanswered chats, overdue follow-ups and leads lost to "no response".

## 25 WhatsApp Follow-Up Message Templates

Replace the words in square brackets. Where a message is likely to be sent more than 24 hours after the customer's last message, a **template version** is included, written with numbered variables so you can submit it for approval on the Business Platform.

### First reply to a new enquiry

**1. Instant reply to a website or ad enquiry**

> Hi [Name], thanks for your enquiry about [product] on our website. I'm [Your name] from [Company]. Is now a good time for a quick 5-minute call, or would you prefer I share details here?

**2. Reply to a lead from IndiaMART, Justdial or a property portal**

> Hi [Name], I saw your enquiry for [product / property] on [portal]. We have [one key detail, such as 3 options in your budget]. Shall I send the details here?

**3. After-hours reply**

> Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out to [Company]. Our team is offline right now, but [Your name] will message you by 10 am tomorrow. If it's urgent, call [number].

**4. Qualifying questions**

> Thanks, [Name]. So I can send the right options: roughly how many [units / users / sq ft] do you need, and by when?

### After a missed call

**5. You missed their call**

> Hi [Name], sorry I missed your call. I'm free now, or let me know a time that suits you today.

**6. They did not pick up**

> Hi [Name], this is [Your name] from [Company]. I tried calling about your enquiry for [product]. When's a good time to talk? Happy to chat here too.

### After sharing a brochure, price or quote

**7. Brochure or catalogue sent**

> Hi [Name], I've shared the [brochure / catalogue] above. Page [3] has the models in your budget. Want me to shortlist two for you?

**8. Checking the quote, next day**

> Hi [Name], did the quote for [product] cover everything you need, or should I add [delivery / installation]?

**9. Quote with no reply after a few days**

> Hi [Name], sharing the quote for [product] again here so it's easy to find. Any questions I can answer?

**Template version:** Hi \{\{1\}\}, this is \{\{2\}\} from [Company]. Just checking whether you had a chance to review quote \{\{3\}\}. Reply here if you have any questions.

**10. Following up on a price concern**

> Hi [Name], I checked with my team. We can do [smaller first order / payment in two parts / a different model] to bring the cost down. Shall I send the revised quote?

### Before and after a meeting, demo or site visit

**11. Reminder the day before**

> Hi [Name], looking forward to seeing you tomorrow at [time] at [place]. Here's the location pin. Reply here if anything changes.

**Template version (utility):** Hi \{\{1\}\}, this is a reminder of your \{\{2\}\} with [Company] on \{\{3\}\}. Reply to this message if you need to reschedule.

**12. Thank you after a site visit or meeting**

> Hi [Name], thanks for visiting today. As discussed, [summary in one line]. I'll call you on [day] to confirm the next step. Any questions before then?

**13. After a demo**

> Hi [Name], thanks for your time on the demo. Here's the recording and the pricing we discussed. Which of the two plans felt closer to what you need?

**14. Bringing in the decision maker**

> Hi [Name], if it helps, I'm happy to join a short call with [partner / manager / family] to answer their questions directly. Would this week work?

### When the lead goes quiet

**15. A one-line yes or no**

> Hi [Name], quick one: are you still looking for [product], or should I close this for now?

**Template version:** Hi \{\{1\}\}, are you still interested in \{\{2\}\}? Reply YES and I'll share the latest options, or NO and we won't follow up on this.

**16. Adding value with an example**

> Hi [Name], we recently set up [product] for a [similar business] in [city]. They were worried about [concern] too. Happy to share how it went.

**17. A real deadline**

> Hi [Name], prices on [model] go up on [date]. If you'd like, I can hold the current rate for you until [day].

**18. Closing the loop politely**

> Hi [Name], I haven't heard back, so I'll assume the timing isn't right and close this for now. If anything changes, just reply here and I'll pick it up.

**Template version:** Hi \{\{1\}\}, we haven't heard back about \{\{2\}\}, so we'll pause our follow-ups for now. Reply to this message anytime if you'd like to continue.

### Handling common replies

**19. "I'll think about it"**

> Sure, [Name], take your time. Is there one thing you'd like me to clarify that would make the decision easier?

**20. "We're comparing other options"**

> Makes sense, [Name]. If it helps, I can send a one-page comparison of what's included with us, so it's easier to check side by side.

**21. "Call me after [date / festival]"**

> Noted, [Name]. I'll message you on [date]. Wishing you and your family a great [festival].

### Re-engaging old leads

These are usually sent weeks or months later, so on the Business Platform they need an approved marketing template.

**22. New stock, model or offer**

**Template version:** Hi \{\{1\}\}, you'd asked us about \{\{2\}\} earlier. We now have \{\{3\}\} available. Reply here if you'd like the details.

**23. Checking in after a few months**

**Template version:** Hi \{\{1\}\}, this is \{\{2\}\} from [Company]. You'd enquired about \{\{3\}\} a while ago. Are you still planning this? Reply here and we'll share current options.

### Payments and after the sale

**24. Payment reminder**

> Hi [Name], a gentle reminder that invoice [number] for [amount] is due on [date]. Here's the payment link. Let me know if you need anything from our side.

**Template version (utility):** Hi \{\{1\}\}, a reminder that invoice \{\{2\}\} for \{\{3\}\} is due on \{\{4\}\}. Please reply here if you have any questions about it.

**25. Review or referral request**

> Hi [Name], hope [product] is working well for you. If you're happy with us, a quick Google review would mean a lot: [link]. And if you know someone who needs [product], we'd be glad to help them too.

## When to Send WhatsApp Follow-Ups

| Situation | First follow-up | Second | Third |
|---|---|---|---|
| New enquiry | Within 5 to 15 minutes | Same day, if no reply | Next morning, then call |
| Quote or brochure sent | Next day | Day 3 or 4 | Day 7, closing message |
| Missed call | Within minutes | Later the same day | Next day |
| Site visit or meeting | Reminder the day before | Thank you the same day | Next step on the agreed date |
| Payment due | On the due date | 3 days after | 7 days after, plus a call |
| Old lead | When you have something new | One month later | Quarterly at most |

Two extra rules: send during business hours unless the customer chose the time, and if they reply, follow the conversation instead of the schedule. For a complete follow-up plan across calls, WhatsApp and email, see our [sales follow-up strategy](/blog/sales-follow-up-strategy).

## WhatsApp Follow-Up Etiquette

- **Do** write the way your customer writes. Hinglish or a regional language is fine if that is how they messaged you.
- **Do** use voice notes for long explanations, but keep them under a minute.
- **Do** send documents as PDFs with clear names, such as "Quote-Sharma-50-chairs.pdf".
- **Don't** send five messages in a row. One clear message beats a stream.
- **Don't** forward long promotional posters to someone who asked a simple question.
- **Don't** add customers to broadcast groups where they can see each other's numbers.
- **Don't** argue over price on chat. Move negotiations to a call.
