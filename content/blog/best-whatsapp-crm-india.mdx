Indian customers already message businesses on WhatsApp, so the real question is how your team keeps up. The best WhatsApp CRM puts every chat next to the lead it belongs to, so nobody replies twice, forgets a follow-up or loses a customer when a salesperson leaves.

But "WhatsApp CRM" covers very different products. Some are WhatsApp inbox and broadcast tools with light contact management. Others are full sales CRMs with WhatsApp built in. They also differ on whether they use Meta's official API, whether they charge per account or per user, and how Meta's message charges reach your bill.

We compared 8 tools that Indian teams shortlist on exactly those points. If your leads still live in chats and a spreadsheet, read [CRM vs Excel](/crm-vs-excel) first, then use this guide to pick the best WhatsApp CRM for how you sell.

## WhatsApp Business App vs WhatsApp Business API

Before comparing tools, decide which side of WhatsApp you need. Meta offers two products, and every tool on this list builds on one or both.

| | WhatsApp Business App | WhatsApp Business Platform (API) |
|---|---|---|
| Who Meta builds it for | Small businesses chatting from a single device | Medium and large businesses messaging at scale |
| Cost | Free to download and use | Charged per delivered message |
| Team access | Up to 4 linked devices, or 10 with multi-agent through Meta Verified or Meta One | Many agents through a CRM or inbox tool |
| Automation | Greeting and away messages, catalog | Approved templates, automated flows, chatbots |
| Broadcasts | Not available on linked devices | Template broadcasts at scale |
| CRM integration | Limited | Built for integration with CRMs |

The Business App is enough for one or two people answering enquiries. Once several agents share one number, or you need to send template messages to many customers, you need the Platform, either directly or through a Business Solution Provider. The best WhatsApp CRM for you depends on which of the two your team needs.

### What Meta charges in 2026

- **Per message, not per conversation.** Since 1 July 2025, Meta charges per delivered message, based on the category (marketing, utility, authentication or service) and the recipient's country.
- **Service messages from 1 October 2026.** Meta now charges for service messages too, after the first 1,000 free service messages per business phone number each month.
- **Free entry points.** Messages sent after a customer writes in from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad or a Facebook Page button are free for 72 hours.
- **Partner fees are separate.** Meta has no fee to access the platform directly, but partners may charge for their services. If you use a Solution Provider, Meta bills the provider, who bills you.

That last point matters. Even the best WhatsApp CRM can cost more than expected if its message rates are high, so compare both the subscription and the per-message rate each provider passes on.

## 2. Interakt

Interakt is a WhatsApp marketing and commerce platform that also sells a separate Sales CRM plan for teams that want pipelines.

**Key features**

- Shared team inbox on all plans, one-time and ongoing campaigns, and a chatbot builder
- WhatsApp catalog, checkout bot, order panel and WhatsApp Pay from Growth
- Sales CRM plan with sales pipelines, auto-assignment of new WhatsApp leads and custom sales reports
- Integrations with Shopify, Razorpay, WooCommerce and Google Sheets

**Pricing:** 14-day free trial. Starter is ₹999 a month per account (₹11,999 a year), Growth ₹2,799 and Advanced ₹3,799 a month, plus taxes. The Sales CRM plan is ₹2,499 a month with 5 sales agents, and ₹499 for each extra agent.

**Pros**

- Official API through a Meta Business Partner
- Strong commerce features for D2C brands
- Template rates listed on the pricing page, by plan

**Cons**

- The pipeline needs a separate Sales CRM plan
- Prices on the page should be confirmed in a browser before you buy, because the page loads them with scripts

**Official site:** [Interakt pricing](https://www.interakt.shop/pricing/)

## How to Choose the Best WhatsApp CRM for Your Business

1. **Pick App or API first.** One or two people answering enquiries can manage with the Business App. Several agents on one number, or template broadcasts, need the official API.
2. **Decide if you need a pipeline.** If WhatsApp brings leads you have to close, choose a tool that tracks deals, not just contacts. Our guide to [lead management software](/lead-management-software) explains how capture, assignment and follow-up fit together.
3. **Compare per-account and per-user pricing.** Inbox tools like WATI, AiSensy and Interakt include a set number of users. CRMs like TracktCRM, Bigin, Zoho CRM and TeleCRM charge per user.
4. **Ask how Meta charges are billed.** Check whether the provider passes Meta's rates through or adds a margin, and budget for service messages beyond the 1,000 free each month.
5. **Match your industry.** Real estate teams, for example, juggle portal leads, site visits and brokers on WhatsApp. Our [real estate CRM](/industries/real-estate-crm) page shows that workflow.
6. **Test before you pay.** Start on a free plan or trial with real leads for a week and see whether your team actually uses it.
