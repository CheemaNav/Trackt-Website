## What is a CRM? (Quick answer)

A CRM (customer relationship management) system is software that keeps every lead and customer in one place, with their full history of calls, messages, quotes and follow-ups. It tells your team who to contact next and shows the owner where every deal stands.

The three main types of CRM are:

- **Operational CRM:** runs daily sales work: lead capture, assignment, follow-ups, pipelines. Most small business CRMs are this type.
- **Analytical CRM:** turns customer data into reports, such as which lead source converts best.
- **Collaborative CRM:** shares customer information across sales, support and other teams.

Modern CRMs, including TracktCRM, combine all three in one tool.

## 15 CRM software examples by business type

Each of these CRM software examples follows the same format: the problem the business had, what the CRM does about it and the pipeline stages it uses.

### 1. Real estate broker

**Problem:** enquiries arrive from 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, Facebook ads and WhatsApp. Agents reply late and forget to call back after site visits.

**What the CRM does:** captures every portal lead automatically, assigns it to an agent by project or location, and reminds them before and after each site visit.

**Pipeline stages:** New enquiry → Contacted → Site visit scheduled → Site visit done → Negotiation → Booked / Lost.

Read more: [real estate CRM](/industries/real-estate-crm).

### 2. Real estate builder with channel partners

**Problem:** the same buyer is brought by two brokers, and nobody can prove who sourced the lead first.

**What the CRM does:** records which channel partner added each lead and when, so commission disputes are settled from data, not arguments.

**Pipeline stages:** Lead registered by partner → Verified → Site visit → Booking → Agreement.

### 3. Car or two-wheeler dealership

**Problem:** walk-ins and test-drive requests are written in a register; most never get a second call.

**What the CRM does:** logs every walk-in and online enquiry, books test drives, and reminds sales staff to follow up until purchase.

**Pipeline stages:** Enquiry → Test drive → Quotation → Finance / exchange → Booked → Delivered.

Read more: [automotive CRM](/industries/crm-for-automotive).

### 4. Coaching institute or college admissions

**Problem:** hundreds of enquiries in admission season, counsellors in different branches, and parents asking the same questions again.

**What the CRM does:** routes each enquiry to a counsellor, schedules demo classes and campus visits, and keeps the full conversation history with the student and parent.

**Pipeline stages:** Enquiry → Counselling call → Demo class / visit → Application → Fee paid → Enrolled.

Read more: [education CRM](/industries/education-crm).

### 5. Clinic or hospital

**Problem:** patients enquire on the website, Practo, Google and WhatsApp, and the front desk misses calls during busy hours.

**What the CRM does:** brings every patient enquiry into one list, assigns it to staff, and sends appointment and follow-up reminders.

**Pipeline stages:** Enquiry → Called back → Appointment booked → Visited → Follow-up due.

Read more: [healthcare CRM](/industries/crm-for-healthcare).

### 6. Diagnostic centre or lab

**Problem:** home-collection requests come in by phone and chat with no tracking of who confirmed the slot.

**What the CRM does:** turns each request into a lead with a time slot, an assigned person and a status.

**Pipeline stages:** Request → Slot confirmed → Sample collected → Report shared.

### 7. Insurance agent or POSP

**Problem:** renewal dates live in a diary, and missed renewals mean lost income every year.

**What the CRM does:** stores every policy with its due date and reminds the agent before each renewal; quote follow-ups run until the client decides.

**Pipeline stages:** Lead → Needs discussed → Quote sent → Policy issued → Renewal due.

Read more: [insurance CRM](/industries/crm-for-insurance).

### 8. Loan agent or DSA

**Problem:** each loan file waits on documents from the customer and approvals from the bank, and status updates live in WhatsApp.

**What the CRM does:** tracks each file stage by stage with document checklists and reminders, so no file sits idle.

**Pipeline stages:** Lead → Eligibility checked → Documents collected → Logged in with bank → Sanctioned → Disbursed.

### 9. Travel agency

**Problem:** holiday season enquiries pile up faster than the team can send itineraries.

**What the CRM does:** captures enquiries from ads, the website and WhatsApp, assigns them to an agent, and follows up until the trip is booked.

**Pipeline stages:** Enquiry → Itinerary sent → Revised quote → Booked → Travelled.

Read more: [travel CRM](/industries/crm-for-travel).

### 10. Digital marketing agency

**Problem:** proposals, retainers and referrals are scattered across email, WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets.

**What the CRM does:** tracks every pitch from first call to signed retainer, with reminders for proposal follow-ups and renewals.

**Pipeline stages:** Lead → Discovery call → Proposal sent → Negotiation → Won → Retainer renewal.

Read more: [CRM for agencies](/industries/crm-for-agencies).

### 11. Recruitment firm

**Problem:** two pipelines at once, clients with open roles and candidates for those roles, and both go cold quickly.

**What the CRM does:** keeps client mandates and candidates in separate pipelines with follow-up reminders on both sides.

**Pipeline stages (candidates):** Sourced → Screened → Shared with client → Interview → Offer → Joined.

Read more: [recruitment CRM](/industries/crm-for-recruitment).

### 12. B2B manufacturer or trader

**Problem:** IndiaMART, TradeIndia and Justdial leads arrive all day, and most are never answered.

**What the CRM does:** pulls these leads in automatically, assigns them by product or region, and tracks quotes and samples until the order.

**Pipeline stages:** Enquiry → Requirement confirmed → Quotation → Sample → Order → Repeat order.

### 13. Freelancer or consultant

**Problem:** a handful of high-value enquiries, but forgetting one follow-up costs a full month's income.

**What the CRM does:** a simple pipeline with reminders, so every enquiry gets a quote and every quote gets a follow-up.

**Pipeline stages:** Enquiry → Call → Proposal → Won → Repeat work.

### 14. Online store or D2C brand

**Problem:** customers ask questions on WhatsApp and Instagram before buying, and bulk or reseller enquiries get lost among them.

**What the CRM does:** separates high-value enquiries such as bulk orders into a pipeline and keeps chat history on each contact.

**Pipeline stages:** Enquiry → Quote → Payment link sent → Paid → Repeat customer.

### 15. Coworking space or service business

**Problem:** visit requests and membership enquiries are handled by whoever picks up the phone.

**What the CRM does:** books tours, tracks each enquiry to signed agreement and reminds staff about renewals.

**Pipeline stages:** Enquiry → Tour booked → Proposal → Agreement signed → Renewal due.

## How to use these CRM software examples to pick a CRM

1. **Find your business in the examples above** and write down your pipeline stages.
2. **List your lead sources.** The CRM must capture them automatically, or your team will not use it.
3. **Check mobile use.** If your team sells in the field, test the CRM on a phone first.
4. **Start free.** Use a free plan with real leads for two weeks before paying.
5. **Count total cost per user,** including any paid add-ons for WhatsApp or integrations.
