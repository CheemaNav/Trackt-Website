## Rules for every post

- Frontmatter: title, slug, metaTitle (≤60), metaDescription (140–155), primaryKeyword, category, excerpt, publishedAt, updatedAt, heroImage, heroAlt, faqs, relatedPages, draft.
- Primary keyword in H1, metaTitle, metaDescription, first 100 words and 2+ H2s; density 0.8–1.2%.
- Competitor facts only from official pricing, feature or help pages, with URL and check date in a Sources section.
- TracktCRM facts only from /pricing and features confirmed live in the dashboard code. AI is on Premium and All-in-One only.
- Keyword notes go in `seo-research/blog/<slug>.md` (not committed).
